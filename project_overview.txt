================================================================================
                        VEXORA AI: LANGUAGE LEARNING APP
                               PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Vexora AI is a playful, interactive language learning application (similar to 
Duolingo) that lets you learn new languages through simple lessons, dynamic 
quizzes, and conversation practice. It has your personal AI language coach, 
Vexora, built right in to chat with you and help you practice speaking and 
listening naturally.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE APP FLOW (HOW A LEARNER EXPERIENCES THE APP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Getting Started:
  When you open the app, you are welcomed by a simple onboarding screen. You can
  instantly create an account or sign in. Next, you select the path language you 
  want to learn (Spanish, French, or Japanese).

* Daily Dashboard (Home):
  This is your control center. Here, you see:
  - Your Flag: The language you are currently practicing.
  - Streak Counter: A fire symbol tracking how many days in a row you have 
    practiced. If you practice, it grows; if you miss a day, it resets.
  - Daily Goal Card: Shows your daily XP (Experience Points) goal progress (e.g. 
    aiming for 20 XP a day) with a progress bar and a visual treasure chest.
  - Continue Learning: A prominent button that shows your current active lesson 
    and lets you jump right back into practice.

* The Learning Hub (Learn Tab):
  This tab organizes your structured exercises. You can toggle between:
  - Flashcards: Practice specific vocabulary words. You swipe and tap cards to 
    see translations, hear the correct pronunciation, and tap a microphone to 
    practice saying the word. The app checks if your accent is correct. If you 
    mispronounce a word (under 50% score), the app transcribes exactly what it 
    sounded like you said and displays a helpful correction to guide your accent.
  - Quizzes: Test your skills across different categories. You face a variety 
    of question formats (like multiple-choice, matching pairs, translation, and 
    listening) with an animated mascot celebrating when you pass a quiz.

* AI Chat Coach (Chat Tab):
  Simulate real-life conversations (like ordering at a cafe or checking in at 
  an airport). 
  - Vexora writes to you in the target language.
  - You can instantly translate her messages or listen to them out loud.
  - To make typing easy, the app displays dynamic "Quick Reply Suggestions" 
    at the bottom of the screen. You can tap a suggestion to instantly reply, 
    complete the conversation, and earn +15 XP.

* Real-Time Voice Calls (AI Teacher Tab) - *LATEST UPDATE*:
  This tab allows you to practice speaking live in a conversational, hands-free 
  voice session. 
  - Study Room: You enter a cozy virtual room where your teacher mascot stands 
    ready to practice with you.
  - Initiating a Call: Tap the phone icon to start a voice call.
  - Speaking & Listening: Vexora speaks to you. A floating speech bubble displays
    what she said, and you can press the Speaker button to hear it again.
  - Subtitles & Translations: You can toggle translations on or off to help you 
    understand what she's saying. During the listening phase, the translation 
    is shown directly inside the bubble to guide you as you prepare to respond.
  - Dynamic Mascot Expressions: Vexora's facial expressions change dynamically based 
    on your pronunciation! While explaining concepts or listening, she appears in 
    focused teacher mode. When you speak, she evaluates you: she smiles and waves 
    when you score high (75% or above), shows a slightly disappointed face if you 
    score moderately (50% to 74%), and looks completely sad if you score low 
    (under 50%).
  - Interactive Microphone: When it's your turn to reply, tap the microphone to 
    speak. The app transcribes and grades your pronunciation.
  - Dialogue Rounds: You practice through a 3-round conversation.
  - Session Results: After 3 rounds, you see a summary. If your average score is 
    60% or higher, you pass and earn a "+30 XP" bonus! If not, the app shows the 
    sad mascot alongside a round-by-round score breakdown so you know where you 
    need more practice, and lets you retry or exit.

* User Profile & Settings (Profile Tab):
  Here you can see your total stats (active language, lessons completed, total XP, 
  and daily streak) and view your achievements (Streak Flame, XP Collector, 
  Wise Scholar). You can also open the Settings menu to switch your target path 
  language, reset all learning progress to start fresh, or sign out.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BEHIND THE SCENES (EXPLAINED FOR A NON-TECHNICAL PERSON)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Smart Pronunciation Assessment:
  The app is smart enough to detect silent audio or background noise. If you submit 
  silence, it won't let you cheat and will record a score of zero, encouraging 
  honest practice.

* Detailed Pronunciation Feedback:
  Rather than simply grading answers as pass/fail, the speech engine calculates an 
  exact percentage match. For lower scores (under 50%), it tells you exactly what 
  it heard (e.g. showing "It sounds like you said 'bonjor' instead of 'bonjour'") 
  so you know exactly which syllables to adjust.

* Premium Audio Transcriptions:
  The app uses the latest premium speech models ("Universal 3 Pro" and 
  "Universal 2") to accurately transcribe what you say. It is designed to be 
  incredibly supportive, meaning it forgives minor accent quirks or close 
  phonetic sounds (like "manger" vs. "mangé" in French) so you don't get 
  penalized for tiny differences.

* Local Progress Memory:
  Your scores, XP progress, and learning achievements are securely saved on your 
  device, ensuring your learning journey is never lost when you close the app.
